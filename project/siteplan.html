Site Name

Lagos Fresh Market

LFM Logo - Fresh Leaf

Domain: lagosfreshmarket.org

I chose Lagos Fresh Market because it is specific to Lagos, easy to remember, and clearly communicates freshness and local market focus. The name builds trust with local shoppers and is perfect for branding and visibility.

Site Purpose

The site provides a hub for fresh farm produce in Lagos by providing product listings with prices, vendor information, seasonal availability, and a direct order/contact form. Services include browsing vegetables, fruits, and grains, saving favorites to a shopping list using localStorage, and contacting vendors for delivery.

Scenarios

  • What is the current price of tomatoes, pepper, and onions at Mile 12 market and can I save them to my shopping list for tomorrow?
  • Where can I find a trusted vendor near Yaba who delivers fresh fruits and how do I place an order online?

Color Schema

Primary #1A5D1A (Deep Green): Used for header, footer, h1, h3, and primary buttons. Represents freshness, farm, nature.

Secondary #FFB534 (Golden Yellow): Used for h2 backgrounds, call-to-action, accents, logo border. Represents Lagos energy and harvest.

#1A5D1A #FFB534

This document uses both colors - header/footer use Primary, h2 uses Secondary as required.

Typography

Montserrat (700): Used for all headings h1, h2, h3 and logo for strong, modern branding.

Open Sans (400): Used for body paragraphs, lists, and all general content for excellent readability on mobile.

This document uses Montserrat for headings and Open Sans for body as shown.
