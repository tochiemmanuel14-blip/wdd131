Lagos Fresh Market - Site Plan

Chimezie Emmanuel - WDD 131

1. Site Name: Lagos Fresh Market

Reason: I chose Lagos Fresh Market because it clearly describes a local marketplace for fresh farm produce in Lagos. It is memorable, searchable, and connects directly with Lagos buyers looking for fresh food.

Domain: lagosfreshmarket.org (Available and affordable)

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Lagos Fresh Market is to provide an online platform where Lagos residents can browse fresh farm products, check prices, see vendor locations, and create a shopping list. The site will include:

  • Product listings with images and prices
  • Vendor information and market locations (Oshodi, Mile 12, Yaba)
  • Shopping list feature using localStorage to save items
  • Contact form for vendor inquiries and order requests
  • Responsive design for mobile market shoppers

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1: A mother in Ikeja wants to check today's price of tomatoes and pepper before going to market - Where can I find current prices for fresh vegetables?

Scenario 2: A student looking for affordable foodstuff near Yaba - Which vendors sell near Yaba and what are their contact details?

4. Color Schema

Primary Color: #1A5D1A (Forest Green) - Used for header background, h2 headings, and primary buttons. Represents freshness and agriculture.

Secondary Color: #FFB534 (Golden Yellow) - Used for h2 bottom borders, highlights, logo border, and call-to-action buttons. Represents harvest and energy.

#1A5D1A #FFB534

5. Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat - Weight 700 Bold - Used for all h1, h2, h3 headings. Chosen for strong, modern, readable headlines.

Sample Heading - Lagos Fresh Market

Body Font: Open Sans - Regular 400 - Used for paragraphs, lists, and body content. Chosen for excellent readability on mobile.

Sample Body - Fresh tomatoes, pepper, and onions available daily at Mile 12 Market from trusted farmers.
